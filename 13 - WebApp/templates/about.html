About The System

What is CardioPredict AI?

CardioPredict AI is a machine learning based web application developed to assist in the early assessment of heart disease risk.

Heart Disease Risk Prediction

The system analyses important patient medical parameters such as age, gender, chest pain type, blood pressure, cholesterol, fasting blood sugar, resting ECG, maximum heart rate, exercise-induced angina, Oldpeak and ST slope.

Machine Learning

A trained machine learning model processes the patient's information and generates an estimated heart disease risk result. The prediction is intended to support educational and research purposes.
